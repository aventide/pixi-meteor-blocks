# Ocean (D08) notes

## What was built

- **Tiles** (`public/assets/planets/ocean/`): original 128×128 glossy tiles in the
  Earth structure (dark outer edge, vertical body gradient, faint bottom caustic
  glow, bright inner rim, top gloss band, white glyph with dark outline and drop
  shadow). Glyphs: red = starfish (with pink dot texture), blue = fish, green =
  branching coral, yellow = scallop shell (amber ridges, dark brown outline for
  contrast). Blue uses a lighter cyan-blue body and a near-white rim so it stands
  out on the deep-blue sea. Burned = matte grey stone with no gloss, a dull rim,
  cracks and specks, and a bleached, cracked coral glyph without tip "polyps".
  Generated by a small Node script (scratchpad, not committed); the SVGs are the
  source of truth.
- **Background** (`createBackground` in `src/themes/ocean.ts`): teal-to-indigo
  water gradient, pulsing surface glow, slanted light shafts that fade with depth
  and sway (rotation + alpha only), rising bubbles of varied sizes with a side
  wobble (position only), and faint kelp/coral silhouettes plus a seabed ridge.
  Everything is drawn in `resize`; `update` only moves/fades.
- **Trail:** `bubbles`, white → pale cyan.
- **Map icon:** blue globe with lighter depth layers, five wavy wave bands, cyan
  glow, rim, shading and a specular highlight.
- **Palette:** accent `#5ee8f2` (aqua), panel `#08223f`, text `#e6fbff`.

## Tuning

| Value             | Earth | Ocean | Why                                                                                                       |
| ----------------- | ----- | ----- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| gravity           | 980   | 1000  | Slightly "heavier" water; HUD still reads 1.0×.                                                           |
| launchVelocity    | -1200 | -1140 | Launches rise ~88% as high (peak ≈650 vs 735 ref px), so more re-ignitions are needed.                    |
| launchDescentRate | 200   | 150   | Launched stacks sink slowly (sluggish), which also gives time to re-ignite and offsets the weaker launch. |
| popVelocity       | -6000 | -6000 | Unchanged; the pop is a UI feel thing, not a difficulty knob.                                             |
| dropIntervalMs    | 700   | 800   | Calmer spawns fit the slow mood and offset the harder launches.                                           |
| goal              | 40    | 45    | A bit above Earth; second planet, only slightly harder.                                                   |

Tagline: "Heavy water slows every launch."
